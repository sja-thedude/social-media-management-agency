Our Process

How we hire (it's quick).

Four steps, never more than three weeks end to end. Same process for every role.

01

Apply

Send us a CV plus a 2–3 minute Loom on why this role. No cover letter needed.

02

Intro call (30 min)

You meet our talent lead. Mutual fit, salary clarity, your questions answered.

03

Paid working session

A 2-hour, paid case study from your future role. No homework, no spec work.

04

Team chat & offer

Meet 2–3 future teammates, ask anything, get an offer (or honest "not this time") within 48 hours.
