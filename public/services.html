FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

How quickly can we get started?

Usually 7–10 days from signed contract. We use the first two weeks for discovery and strategy, then start posting at the end of week three.

Do you require long contracts?

Three-month minimum, then month-to-month. We've never had to chase a renewal — our retention speaks for itself.

Do you handle paid ad spend?

Yes, on Meta & TikTok. Our retainers cover management; ad spend is separate and goes directly on your account. We recommend a minimum of $5K/mo in spend to make paid worthwhile.

Who owns the content you create?

You do. 100% of original content created during the engagement is yours, in perpetuity, in full resolution, with all source files.

Can you work with our existing in-house team?

Absolutely. We act as an extension of in-house teams on about half of our accounts — handing off vs. owning whichever workstreams make sense.

What industries do you specialize in?

DTC, lifestyle, B2B SaaS, and hospitality are our sweet spots. We've turned down accounts in industries we don't think we can do justice to.
